Privacy Policy — Slingshot Dummy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Operated by JakeTeam LLC (jaketeam.com) under JakePlay.com.

1. Free Guest Mode vs. Registered Accounts

  • Free Guest Mode (All Ages): Zero personal data is collected or sent to our servers. Progress is saved strictly on your device in browser local storage.
  • Registered JakePlay Account (Optional, Ages 13+): If you are 13 or older and sign in with your JakePlay account, we sync your unlocked arenas, per-arena tries/bonks stats, best times, and verified leaderboard clears.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA — Players Under 13)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Account registration is restricted to ages 13+. Players under 13 can play Slingshot Dummy anonymously in Free Guest Mode with zero tracking and zero third-party behavioral advertising. Parents may contact privacy@jakeplay.com for immediate assistance.

3. Account Deletion & Contact

You can permanently delete your JakePlay account and all cloud data directly inside the game via Account & Stats → Delete Account or by emailing privacy@jakeplay.com.
